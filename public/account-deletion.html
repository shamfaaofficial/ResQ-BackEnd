Account Deletion Request

Learn how to delete your RESQ account and associated data

📱

How to Delete Your Account

1

Open the RESQ App

Launch the RESQ application on your mobile device and ensure you are logged in to your account.

2

Go to Profile Settings

Tap on your profile icon and navigate to "Settings" or "Account Settings".

3

Select "Delete Account"

Scroll down to find the "Delete Account" option and tap on it.

4

Confirm Your Identity

Enter your password to verify your identity and confirm the deletion request.

5

Account Deleted

Your account will be deactivated immediately. All associated data will be processed for deletion.

ℹ️

If you're unable to access the app, you can request account deletion by contacting our support team via email.

🗑️

Data That Will Be Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data will be permanently removed:

❌ Personal information (name, email, phone number)
❌ Profile picture and account credentials
❌ Saved addresses and location history
❌ Booking and trip history
❌ Wallet balance and transaction history
❌ Ratings and reviews
❌ Notification preferences and FCM tokens
📁

Data Retention Policy

Some data may be retained for legal, regulatory, or security purposes:

⚠️ Transaction records (retained for 7 years for tax/legal compliance)
⚠️ Anonymized analytics data (non-identifiable)
⚠️ Legal dispute records (if applicable, until resolution)
⏱️

Processing Time: Account deletion is processed immediately upon confirmation. Some background data cleanup may take up to 30 days to complete across all systems.
